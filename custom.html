Ask for something made your way

Tell me what is in your head and I will tell you whether I can make it, what it would cost and roughly when it would land. Asking costs nothing and you are not committing to anything by filling this in.

Things I say yes to most weeks

  • Anything in the shop, but in a different colour from the chart of twenty
  • A bigger or smaller hat, for a kitten, a Maine Coon or a small dog
  • A sleeve cut to fit a reader, handheld or console I have not covered
  • A collar sized for a specific neck, dogs very much included
  • Matching sets, usually a hat and a collar out of the same yarn
  • A pattern you have bought yourself, if you send it over with the order

Things I will say no to

  • Copying another maker's finished piece from a photo. I use bought patterns and credit whoever wrote them, and I am not going to knock off somebody else's work.
  • Blankets, jumpers, cardigans and anything that has to fit a grown adult properly. I make small things well and I would rather point you at somebody who does the big stuff.
  • Anything licensed that I would be selling on, like a character from a film. Small nods are fine, straight copies are not.
  • Wholesale runs. It is only me and one hook, so twenty of anything is not going to happen in a hurry.

If you are not sure which side of the line your idea falls on, just ask. I will not be annoyed.

How the money works. This form is only a conversation. Once we have agreed what it is and what it costs, I set up a listing on Etsy just for you and send you the link. You pay there, which means your receipt, your buyer protection and your refund rights all sit in one place. I never ask for a bank transfer.

Outside the UK, allow one to three weeks for post and expect possible customs charges.

Anything that would help

A picture of your cat, your device or the thing you have in mind is worth a paragraph. Instagram, Google Photos, anywhere I can see it. You can also just attach photos when you reply to my email.

Not a test. It saves us both time if I know I am nowhere near.

Last thing
No payment is taken here. I reply with a price first.

What happens after you press send

  1. It lands in my inbox

    Not a team inbox, mine. I read them in the evening usually, so give me a day or two before you assume it went missing. Do check your junk folder, replies sometimes end up there.

  2. I reply with a price and a rough date

    If I need to ask something, I will. If it is not something I can make well, I will tell you that instead of taking your money and hoping.

  3. You decide, with no pressure

    Plenty of people ask and then decide against it. That is completely fine and it does not offend me.

  4. If you go ahead, I list it for you

    A private listing on Etsy in your name. You pay there, I start making, and you can cancel within 24 hours of ordering if you change your mind.
