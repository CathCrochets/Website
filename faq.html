Delivery and returns

How long will my order take?

Two numbers, added together.

Making it takes up to ten days, because nothing is sitting in a box waiting. If I am quiet it will be quicker, and if it is October and everybody wants a pumpkin hat it will be the full ten.

Then it has to travel. Inside the UK it goes second class Royal Mail. Orders to the USA and Canada go tracked and usually take one to three weeks. Everywhere else sits somewhere in that range too.

If you have a date to hit, a birthday or Christmas, tell me when you order rather than after. I will always tell you honestly whether it will make it.

Do you post internationally?

Yes. I stopped for a while and started again in September 2025.

Postage costs more than it used to because of tariffs, which is genuinely not my doing and not something I make anything on. What you see at checkout is what it costs to send.

Will I get charged customs or import fees?

If you are in the USA, no. Etsy requires import duties and fees to be included in the checkout price, so what you pay at checkout is the lot.

If you are elsewhere outside the UK, you may be asked to pay import charges when it is delivered. That depends on your country's rules rather than on me. Message me before you order if you want me to check what is likely for you.

I cannot mark a parcel as a gift or write down the value. It is not worth either of us getting into trouble over.

Can I cancel or change my order?

You can cancel within 24 hours of buying, no questions asked.

After that I have usually started, and since everything is made for you specifically it cannot go back on a shelf. That said, if something has genuinely gone wrong, message me. I am a person, not a returns department.

Changing a colour or a size is usually fine if you catch me early. Just send a message as soon as you can.

It arrived and something is not right

Tell me. If a hat does not fit, if a seam has gone, if it is not the colour you picked, I will remake it or refund you.

A photograph helps a lot, especially for fit, because then I can see exactly what needs changing rather than guessing.

Handmade pieces do vary slightly from the photographs. Two hats made from the same pattern are never quite identical, and that is the nature of it rather than a fault. If yours is noticeably different from what you ordered, that is a different matter and I will sort it.

The things themselves

Will a hat fit my cat?

Probably. Hats are made in one regular size, tested on Lola, who is a regular sized adult cat, and the long ties give a fair bit of room either way.

If your cat is a kitten, a Maine Coon, or otherwise not regular sized, send me two measurements before you order and I will make it to fit. It does not cost extra. Here is how to take them.

And if it turns up and it does not fit anyway, I will remake it or refund you.

What yarn do you use?

Most things are made in a 100 percent polyester chenille. It is extremely soft, it does not itch, and it comes in twenty colours. That is the yarn people mean in the reviews when they keep saying "so soft".

Coasters and bookmarks are cotton instead. Cotton takes a hot mug and a wash far better than chenille does.

If you would like something in cotton that is normally chenille, or the other way around, ask and I will price it up.

Is it safe for my cat or dog?

These are dress up items and they need supervising. That is the honest answer.

Stay in the room, take it off before they eat, sleep or go outside, and never leave one on an animal on their own. The collars are decorative and have no quick release buckle, so please do not use one as a real collar and never clip a lead to one.

Watch how they react. Flattened ears or freezing on the spot both mean take it off. The full notes are here.

How do I wash it?

Most of my items would be fine in a machine, but with anything handmade I would always hand wash.

Lukewarm water, a drop of mild detergent, swirl rather than scrub, do not wring it out, press the water into a towel and dry it flat away from radiators and direct sun.

The full care guide is here, including what to do about a snag, which is the one thing worth reading before it happens.

Which Tamagotchis do you make cases for?

Paradise, Uni, Smart, ON, MIX, Pix, and the original P1 and P2. Each shell gets its own pattern so the buttons and screen stay clear.

If yours is not on that list, send me a photo of it next to a ruler and I will work out whether I can cover it.

Will the Switch case fit my original Switch?

No, the case in the shop is cut to the Switch 2 specifically. Ask and I will make one to fit the original, or the Lite, or an OLED.

Worth being clear about what it is for. It is thick chunky yarn and it stops keys and coins scratching the screen inside a bag. It is not a hard case and I would not trust it against a drop onto a hard floor.

Ordering, customs and everything else

Do you take custom orders?

Constantly. Roughly half the shop started as somebody asking for something slightly different.

Different colours, bigger or smaller hats, sleeves for devices I have not covered, collars sized for a specific dog, matching sets. There is a form for it which takes about two minutes, and I come back with a price before anybody has spent anything.

Why do I have to pay on Etsy?

Because Etsy does that part better than I could on my own. You get buyer protection, a proper receipt, a record of the conversation and a way to raise a problem that does not depend on me answering my email.

So this website is where I explain things and take requests, and Etsy is where the money changes hands. For a custom piece I set up a private listing in your name and send you the link.

I will never ask you for a bank transfer. If somebody claiming to be me does, it is not me.

Are the designs yours?

Some are. Others are made from patterns I have bought from other crocheters, and when that is the case I name them on the listing. Knotvivi and PawPawCrochet are two you will see credited in my shop.

If you have bought a pattern yourself and want me to make it up for you, send it over with your request and I will happily do that. What I will not do is copy another maker's finished piece from a photograph.

Can I resell what you make?

No, everything is sold for personal use or as a gift. It is a one person shop and each piece takes hours, so it does not work as something to buy in and sell on.

If you run a shop and want to talk about something, message me and we will have a proper conversation about it rather than me just saying no.

Where are you based?

Newcastle upon Tyne, in the north east of England. Everything is made here and posted from here.

How do I get hold of you?

Whichever is easiest for you. Email cathcrochetsstore@gmail.com, use the form below, message me through Etsy if your question is about an order you have already placed, or send a DM on Instagram.

I read them all myself, usually in the evening, so give me a day or two before you assume it went missing. Do check your junk folder, because replies sometimes land there.
