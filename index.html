Made to order

Ask for it in your colour

Half of what is in the shop started as somebody asking for something slightly different. A bigger hat for a cat with a big head. A sleeve for a reader I had never made one for. A collar long enough for a border collie.

So if the thing in your head is not in the shop, ask. It costs nothing to ask and I will tell you honestly if it is not something I can make well.

  • A colour that is not on the listing, from the chart of twenty
  • A bigger or smaller hat for a kitten, a Maine Coon or a small dog
  • A sleeve sized to a reader, handheld or console I have not covered yet
  • A matching set, usually a hat and a collar in the same yarn

How it goes

  1. You tell me what you are after

    The form takes a couple of minutes. Colours, sizes, when you need it by. If you are not sure what to measure, the size guide walks you through it.

  2. I come back with a price

    Usually within a day or two, since I read these myself. If it is not something I can make properly, I will say so before you have spent anything.

  3. You order on Etsy

    I set up a listing just for you. Payment, receipts and buyer protection all stay in the one place, which is safer for both of us.

  4. I make it and post it

    Up to ten days to make, depending on how busy I am. Then second class Royal Mail inside the UK, and tracked everywhere else.

Who is behind it

Me, a hook, and a very patient cat

I am Cath. I am 28, I have been crocheting for eight years, and I started with amigurumi before I got completely taken over by cat hats. Everything in the shop is made by me at home in Newcastle.

Lola does the modelling. She is thirteen, I have had her since I was a teenager, and she has never once objected to a pumpkin on her head. She is my best friend and she has earned every treat she has been paid in.
