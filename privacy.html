Privacy

What happens to your details when you use this site. Written to be read, not to be skipped.

Last updated 16 August 2026

The short version. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Nobody is tracking you here. The only personal information I hold is what you type into one of the two forms, and I use it to write back to you. I do not sell it, I do not share it for marketing, and you can ask me to delete it whenever you like.

1. Who I am

I am Cath, a sole trader based in Newcastle upon Tyne, England, trading as Cath Crochets. I am the data controller for anything you send through this website, which in plain English means I am the person responsible for looking after it.

You can reach me at cathcrochetsstore@gmail.com. If you would rather not email, you can message me through my Etsy shop.

2. What I collect

Only what you type in. There are two forms on this site and neither of them collects anything you cannot see on the screen.

The custom order form and the question form

  • Your name and email address, so I can reply
  • What you are asking for, and anything you tell me about it
  • Any measurements you choose to send
  • Colour choices, a rough budget and a date you need it by, if you fill those in
  • The country it would be posted to
  • A link to a photograph, if you paste one
  • An Etsy order number, if your question is about an existing order

The mailing list form

  • Your email address
  • Your first name, which is optional and only there so I do not have to start every email with "Hello"

If you email me directly

Then I have your email address and whatever you wrote, same as any other email.

What I never ask for

Card details, bank details, your address, your date of birth, or anything similar. Payment and delivery happen on Etsy, not here, so I have no reason to hold any of that.

3. Why, and on what basis

UK data protection law asks me to say which lawful basis I rely on. Here they are.

Replying to your enquiry and quoting you a price
Because you asked me to. In legal terms this is either taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, or my legitimate interest in running a small shop and answering people who contact me.
Sending you the mailing list
Your consent, given by ticking the box. You can withdraw it at any time using the unsubscribe link on any email I send, or by replying and asking. Withdrawing it does not affect anything sent before.
Keeping records of orders
Legal obligation. HMRC expects business records to be kept for a period after the tax year they relate to.

I do not use your information to build a profile of you, and nothing here involves automated decision making.

4. Who else is involved

I am one person, so a few services do the technical work. These are all of them.

The form service

When you submit a form, the contents are passed to Formspree, which forwards it to my inbox. Formspree acts as a processor on my behalf, meaning it handles the data on my instructions and is not allowed to use it for its own purposes. Formspree is based in the United States, and that transfer is covered by the standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum, which is the usual legal mechanism for sending personal data outside the UK.

If the form service is not connected at the time you use the site, the form falls back to opening your own email app with the details already typed out. In that case nothing is sent anywhere until you press send yourself, and only your own email provider is involved.

My email

My inbox is a Google account, so Google stores my email in the ordinary way that any email provider does.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical information when a page is requested, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. That happens for every visitor to every website and I have no access to those logs. GitHub sets out what it does with them in its privacy statement.

5. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies at all. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

There is also no analytics. I have no idea how many people visit, which pages they read or where they came from, and I have decided I can live with that.

There are no advertising pixels, no social media trackers, no session recording and no fingerprinting.

Everything the page needs, including the fonts and every photograph, is served from this website itself. That is deliberate. It means your browser does not quietly contact Google, a font service or a content network just because you opened a page, which is the usual way visitors get tracked without noticing.

The site does use your browser's address bar to remember which shop filter you picked, so a filtered view can be shared as a link. That is stored in the web address you can see and nowhere else.

6. Buying on Etsy

Links to my shop are ordinary links. Nothing is shared with Etsy by you clicking one, beyond what your browser sends to any site you visit.

Once you are on Etsy you are on their website and under their rules. If you buy something, Etsy is a separate data controller in its own right and handles your payment and delivery details. I only see what Etsy shows a seller, which is your name, your delivery address and what you ordered. Their privacy policy covers that side of it.

The same goes for Instagram and X. Following a link there puts you on their site, subject to their policies.

7. How long I keep it

  • An enquiry that does not turn into an order. Up to twelve months, then deleted. I keep them that long because people often come back around a birthday or Christmas and it saves you explaining it twice.
  • Anything connected to an actual order. Six years after the end of the tax year it falls in, because that is what HMRC expects of business records.
  • Mailing list. Until you unsubscribe, and then I remove you.

If you want something gone sooner than that, ask me and I will delete it, unless it is part of a record I am legally required to keep.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me to:

  • Tell you what I hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete it
  • Stop using it, or restrict what I do with it
  • Hand it over in a portable format
  • Stop sending you the mailing list, at any time and without giving a reason

Email me and I will sort it. There is no charge and I will respond within one month, which is the legal limit, though in practice it will be a lot sooner because there is not much to look through.

If you think I have handled your information badly, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so the connection between your browser and it is encrypted. Form submissions travel over an encrypted connection too. My accounts have two factor authentication turned on.

No one can promise perfect security, and I am not going to pretend otherwise. What I can say is that the amount of personal information involved here is deliberately small, which is the most effective protection there is.

10. Children

This site is not aimed at children and I do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 16. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch on your behalf. If you believe a child has sent me their details, email me and I will delete them.

11. Changes to this page

If I change how any of this works, I will update this page and change the date at the top. If it is a significant change and I have your email because you are on the mailing list, I will tell you directly rather than quietly editing a web page.

12. Getting in touch

Any question about this page, or about anything I hold on you, goes to cathcrochetsstore@gmail.com. I would much rather answer a question than have somebody worrying about it.

This policy covers cathcrochets.github.io/Website only. It does not cover Etsy, Instagram or X, each of which has its own.
