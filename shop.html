Not seeing it?

Before you order

  • Check the size guide if you are buying for a cat, a dog or a device
  • Pick a colour from the chart, there are twenty and most things can be made in any of them
  • Allow up to ten days for me to make it, plus postage on top
  • Have a read of the care notes, these are handmade and they like a gentle wash
